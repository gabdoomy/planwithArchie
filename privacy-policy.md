# Privacy Policy – PlanWithArchie

**Effective date:** 15 November 2026  
**Last updated:** 15 November 2026

## 1. Introduction

Welcome to PlanWithArchie ("we", "our", or "us").

PlanWithArchie is a wedding seating planner application that helps users organise wedding guests, manage guest lists and create seating arrangements.

We respect your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how your information is handled when you use PlanWithArchie.

## 2. Information We Collect

**We do not collect personal information through the current version of PlanWithArchie.**

All information entered into the app, including:
- Guest names and details.
- Wedding information and event details.
- Table names and seating arrangements.
- Guest groups, relationships and notes.
- Dietary requirements and preferences.

is stored locally on your device.

This information is not transmitted to our servers or shared with us.

## 3. Data Storage

All information created within PlanWithArchie is stored locally on your device.

We do not currently operate user accounts, cloud synchronisation or remote databases for storing your wedding information.

Your data remains on your device unless you choose to share or export it using functionality provided by your device or the app.

Deleting the application may also delete locally stored app data, depending on your device's operating system and backup settings.

## 4. Third-Party Services

The current version of PlanWithArchie does not use third-party services to collect or process your personal information.

If this changes in future versions, this Privacy Policy will be updated accordingly.

## 5. Data Sharing

We do not collect, sell, rent, share or transmit your personal information to third parties.

Any information you choose to share or export from the application is handled through the sharing functionality and services you select.

## 6. Data Security

Since your information is stored locally on your device, its security is also dependent on the security of your device.

We recommend using appropriate device security measures, such as a passcode or biometric authentication, to protect your information.

## 7. Children's Privacy

PlanWithArchie is not specifically designed for children under the age of 13.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## 8. Future Changes

We may introduce additional features in future versions of PlanWithArchie, such as user accounts, cloud synchronisation, email collection or anonymous usage statistics.

If these features are introduced, we will update this Privacy Policy to explain what information is collected, how it is used, how it is stored and what choices are available to users.

Any new data collection will be subject to applicable privacy and data protection legislation.

## 9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to the application or applicable legal requirements.

The latest version will always be made available at this URL, together with the updated effective date.
